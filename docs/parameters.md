# Parameters

Every number the model is set with: in each region's settings and among the model's own constants. Generated by `tools/params_doc.py` from the files themselves, so it lists what runs. The note is the file's own comment beside or above the number, where its source is given; `ref:` keys link to [references.md](references.md). When a number was set, and why it changed, is in the file's history (click its line, then *History*). Places, names and station codes are left out. Do not edit by hand.

### blooms.toml

| Where | Setting | Value | Note and source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [45](../regions/sf-bay-delta/blooms.toml#L45) | `temperature_c` | `[19.0, 25.0]` | Water temperature, degC, the day's mean. Microcystis is "not observed at temperatures <19 degC" in the Delta, and "requires temperatures above 25 degC for growth rates to be competitive with diatoms" (Berg and Sutula 2015, [bergsutula2015](references.md#bergsutula2015), Table 5.1, p. 49). Below the first: unlikely; between: possible; above the second: likely. Bouma-Gregson et al. 2024 (Fig. 6), from the surveys' visual index 2014-2021, find high ratings climbing steeply above 25 degC. |
| [52](../regions/sf-bay-delta/blooms.toml#L52) | `salinity_below_psu` | `5.0` | Salinity, psu, the day's mean. Microcystis colonies were found at 0.1 to 18 ppt, but bloom biomass only in Delta water below 5 ppt (Lehman et al. 2005, [lehman2005](references.md#lehman2005), pp. 93-94); salinity sets how far seaward blooms reach, not where within the Delta (Berg and Sutula 2015, p. 48). At or above it: unlikely, whatever the temperature. |
