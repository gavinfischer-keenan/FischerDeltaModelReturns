# The maths

What calculation runs in each kind of water, part by part, where each comes from, and
the code that runs it. Generated by `tools/maths_doc.py` from the register of maths
(`python/fdmr_plugins/registry.py`), which each published cycle also records reach by
reach (`index.json`, `reaches[].maths`). A region may choose another registered
implementation of a part; an unknown choice is refused. Do not edit by hand.

## river-routing

Rivers, from a dam's release down past gauges (one dimension along the channel)

| Part | Maths | What it does | Source | Code |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| flow | **gauge-blend-lag-route** | Flow between two gauges as a blend of their records, each shifted by the flood wave's travel time; beyond the last gauge, variable-parameter lag-and-route (a delay set by flow, then a linear reservoir) | Specification 7.1; lag-and-route as in standard hydrological routing texts (e.g. Chow, Maidment and Mays 1988, Applied Hydrology, ch. 8; [chow1988](references.md#chow1988)) | `fdmr_plugins.river_routing.model, crates/fdmr-math routing.rs` |
| channel | **power-law-hydraulic-geometry** | Cross-section area and top width as power laws of flow, fitted to each gauge's recent USGS channel measurements | Leopold and Maddock 1953, USGS Professional Paper 252 (hydraulic geometry; [leopold1953](references.md#leopold1953)) | `fdmr_plugins.river_routing.channel` |
| travel-time | **measured-release-waves** | Wave scale and water factor per segment, from release changes measured arriving at the next gauge, refitted nightly | Specification 9.1 | `fdmr_plugins.river_routing.calibration, wave` |
| temperature | **surface-heat-budget** | The release's measured temperature carried down at the water's speed, with the surface heat budget (sunlight, sky and water long-wave, evaporation, conduction) under each element's nearest airport's weather, corrected toward temperature stations | Chapra 1997, Surface Water-Quality Modeling, Lecture 30 ([chapra1997](references.md#chapra1997)); cloud term as QUAL2K ([chapra2008qual2k](references.md#chapra2008qual2k)); FAO-56 clear-sky sunlight ([allen1998fao56](references.md#allen1998fao56)) | `fdmr_plugins.river_routing.heat, fdmr_runner.publish river_temperature` |
| dispersion | **fischer-longitudinal** | Longitudinal dispersion coefficient from width, depth, velocity and shear velocity | Fischer, List, Koh, Imberger and Brooks 1979, Mixing in Inland and Coastal Waters, ch. 5 ([fischer1979](references.md#fischer1979)) | `crates/fdmr-math dispersion.rs` |
| release | **held-or-rule** | A dam's release ahead: the River Forecast Center's forecast where given; else following its release forecast upstream; else the flood-control rule (storage against the Corps' top of conservation); else held | US Army Corps of Engineers daily Flood Control Requirements ([usace_fcr](references.md#usace_fcr)); NOAA CNRFC ([cnrfc](references.md#cnrfc)) | `fdmr_runner.releases` |

## 1d-tidal-network

A tidal channel network, such as the Delta (DSM2, one dimension per channel)

| Part | Maths | What it does | Source | Code |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| flow | **dsm2-hydro** | One-dimensional unsteady flow in a channel network (St Venant equations, four-point implicit scheme) | California DWR, DSM2 HYDRO (v8.5.1; [dsm2](references.md#dsm2)), run unchanged on live inputs | `fdmr_plugins.dsm2.study, fdmr_runner.delta` |
| salinity | **dsm2-qual-ec** | Advection-dispersion of conductance (EC) along the channels and through junctions and reservoirs | California DWR, DSM2 QUAL ([dsm2](references.md#dsm2)) | `fdmr_plugins.dsm2.study` |
| temperature | **dsm2-qual-heat** | QUAL's surface heat budget for every channel under the region's weather | California DWR, DSM2 QUAL (heat; [dsm2](references.md#dsm2)) | `fdmr_plugins.dsm2.temperature` |
| water-sources | **qual-fingerprint** | Each inflow group a conservative tracer: its share of the water everywhere | DWR's fingerprinting method, with DSM2 QUAL ([dsm2](references.md#dsm2)) | `fdmr_plugins.dsm2.fingerprint` |
| sediment | **gtm-cohesive-settled-bed** | Suspended mud carried on HYDRO's flows by DSM2's General Transport Model; deposition ws*c, erosion M(tau_b/tau_ce - 1) (Partheniades) from a bed that holds only what has settled on it; bed shear from Manning's n; inflow SSC from gauges, turbidity through published Delta relations | Achete, van der Wegen, Roelvink and Jaffe 2015 (set-up and calibrated values; [achete2015](references.md#achete2015)); Partheniades 1965 ([partheniades1965](references.md#partheniades1965)); California DWR, DSM2 GTM v8.5.1 ([dsm2](references.md#dsm2)) with runner/dsm2/gtm-sediment.patch | `fdmr_plugins.dsm2.sediment, fdmr_runner.delta ssc_series` |

## 2d-field

An open bay, on a mesh (two dimensions, depth-averaged)

| Part | Maths | What it does | Source | Code |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| flow | **noaa-fvcom-forecast** | NOAA's operational FVCOM forecast for the bay, depth-averaged and coarsened | NOAA CO-OPS operational forecast system ([noaa_ofs](references.md#noaa_ofs); FVCOM: Chen, Liu and Beardsley 2003, [chen2003fvcom](references.md#chen2003fvcom)) | `fdmr_plugins.fvcom` |
| outflow-correction | **network-outflow-match** | The mesh's net flow across its join made to carry the network's | Specification 7.4 | `fdmr_plugins.fvcom.correct` |
| extension | **tides-plus-outflow-fit** | Past NOAA's 48 hours: at each node a least-squares fit of tidal constituents plus river outflow | Harmonic analysis as in Pawlowicz, Beardsley and Lentz 2002 (T_TIDE; [pawlowicz2002](references.md#pawlowicz2002)), extended with an outflow term; constituent speeds from Schureman 1958 ([schureman1958](references.md#schureman1958)) | `fdmr_plugins.fvcom.extend` |
| quality-correction | **station-misfit-spread** | Each station's daily misfit spread along the water by inverse distance squared; left-out checks | Specification 7.5 | `fdmr_runner.bay correct_quality, fdmr_plugins.fvcom.correct` |
