Wherever a value is filled between readings, spread over a day, carried on into the future or taken from somewhere other than a measurement, it is listed here, with why, the code that does it, and the work that would replace it with data. Nothing is filled silently. Reviewed against the code on 26 Nov 2026. See also every published check.

1. Between readings: putting measurements on the model's 15-minute steps

1.1RiversEvery gauge's flow (releases and gauges below them)Each reading is put on its nearest step; between readings the last one is held (a step, not a ramp) until the next. Before the first reading, the first is held back. A gauge that posts only when its flow changes (CDEC "event" series: Camanche, Yuba, Merced, Stanislaus, the Feather gauges) therefore stays a step.A release is changed in steps; a straight line would turn a sudden change into a slow one.river_routing/wave.py regular, river_routing/model.py filled—
1.2Delta (DSM2)Daily figures: pumping at Banks, Jones, Barker Slough and Rock Slough; Camanche's release; Vernalis conductanceEach day's figure is held flat through its day (Pacific Standard Time), as in DWR's own inputs. It is there for places where a release or pumping is known to follow the clock (elsewhere in the world, dams that release only by day).Only daily figures are published live.fdmr_runner/delta.py grid, day_weightsA sourced hourly shape, if one is ever found (an operator's schedule, or an hourly record)

3. The future: carrying each input on past its last reading

#WhereDataWhat is assumedWhyCodeTo replace it
3.1RiversDam releasesWhere the River Forecast Center forecasts the river at the release (the Cosumnes, the Stanislaus), its forecast. The Yuba's release follows the Center's release forecast for New Bullards Bar upstream (its changes added to today's). The Sacramento (Shasta), American (Folsom), Feather (Oroville) and Calaveras (New Hogan) releases follow the flood-control rule: storage is stepped on with the Center's inflow forecast and today's release, and once it would reach the Corps' top of conservation (that day's value, held through the forecast) the release rises to at least the inflow; encroached space is not drawn down faster. Every other release (Bear, Mokelumne, Tuolumne, Merced, San Joaquin, Cache), the Yolo Bypass's flow at Woodland (the Center forecasts Fremont Weir and Lisbon, not Woodland), and Putah Creek's release from the Putah Diversion Dam (CDEC PTF), is held at its last value. Each cycle's own record says which applied (check page, section 0).No operator publishes a release schedule for these gauges.runner/releases.py, adapters/usace_spk.py, forecast.py extendOperators' own release schedules; how fast encroachment is evacuated (each Water Control Manual)
3.2RiversGauges with a River Forecast Center forecastThe forecast, joined to the measured flow over the first day (the difference fades out linearly); past the forecast's end, its last value is held.Forecast and gauge rarely agree at the start.forecast.py extend—
3.3River temperatureRelease temperatureHeld at its last 25-hour mean.No release-temperature forecast exists.publish.py river_temperatureReservoir cold-water pools (#9's list)
3.4River temperature, DeltaWeatherThe Weather Service's forecast (about 7 days), joined over a day, then its last day repeated to the end; air pressure has no forecast and is held at its 25-hour mean.The forecast ends before ours does.delta.py weather, forecast.py repeat_last_day—
3.5Delta (DSM2)The tide at MartinezNOAA's predictions, fitted to the gauge's last week for timing, size and level. Surge is not forecast.Predictions are tide alone.delta.py carried_on, forecast.py fit_tideThe ocean edge (#18)
3.6Delta (DSM2)Freeport and Vernalis flowsThe River Forecast Center's forecast, joined over a day.—delta.py carried_on—
3.7Delta (DSM2)Everything else: pumping, other inflows, gatesHeld at the last value; a tidal signal (Martinez conductance) held at its 25-hour mean; gates held in their last state.No forecast is published for them.delta.py carried_onPublished pumping and gate schedules
3.8Delta (DSM2)Island water useDWR's 2024 averages, not measured.Not measured live.DSM2 study inputsOpenET (#8; needs an account)
3.9BayBeyond NOAA's 48-hour forecastCarried on to the end of the Delta's forecast by a fit of tides plus outflow drift (refitted daily), driven by the latest measured daily outflow moved by DSM2's forecast change; temperature and salinity held at NOAA's last forecast hour.NOAA's Bay forecast stops at 48 hours.bay.py extension_hours, fvcom/extend.py—
3.10CanalsFlow and quality down the export canalsThe flow is the Delta's own export, the same all along a canal; the quality is the intake's, carried down at the water's travel time and otherwise unchanged to the canal's end (107 km for the two longest): no heating or cooling on the way, no mixing along the canal, nothing settling, growing or decaying, and nothing added or taken off (Bethany Reservoir and the South Bay Aqueduct's take among them). Published with the canals' reach, so it shows below too.No live canal gauges are used; temperature along the canals is not measured live.fdmr_plugins/canal, runner/canals.pyCanal temperature and conductance gauges, where published, to check it

3b. Hindcasts (the nightly regime)

3b.1Nightly hindcastEvery input to the Delta and the rivers, as of five days beforeOnly values timed before the hindcast's moment are read, but as they stand now: a provisional reading revised since, or a value fetched late, is used in its present form. River forecasts are the River Forecast Center's issue in force at the hindcast's time where it was kept (issues are kept from 26 Nov 2026, 60 days); otherwise, and for release rules and weather forecasts, held. The Bay and the canals are not hindcast.The store keeps the latest version of each value; forecast issues are overwritten by the next.runner/nightly.py, publish.py --as-of, delta.py --as-ofKeeping the weather forecast's issues, and each value's versions

4. Shown or recorded, not fed to any model

#WhereDataWhat is assumedWhyCode
4.1MapStorage reservoirs' inflow and release (Shasta, Trinity, Oroville, New Bullards Bar, Folsom, New Melones, Don Pedro, McClure, Millerton)Shown as daily means, not spread over the day. The hourly figures swing with power peaking (Folsom 118 to 2,135 cfs in a day) and CDEC's hourly inflow is worked out from storage changes (even below zero).The daily means match DWR's own report.overlays.toml daily
4.2ChecksSatellite water and land temperature (ECOSTRESS)Only clear pixels 350 m from flagged cloud; a point counts only where its pixel and the eight around it agree in kind (all water, or the place's land).Cloud edges read up to 6 °C cold.adapters/ecostress.py
4.3RecordSoil moisture (SMAP Level 4)An area's mean over its land cells (9 km), partly modelled by NASA (measurements assimilated into a land model).The only gap-free soil moisture.adapters/smap.py
4.5RecordRain and snowmelt per areaRain: the Weather Service's daily analysis (radar adjusted to gauges), an area's mean over its 4.8 km cells. Snowmelt and snow water: SNODAS, modelled (the Weather Service's snow model nudged to observations); where SNODAS gives no melt and no snowpack, no melt is taken. SNODAS and DWR's reviewed sensors can disagree early in the season (26 Nov 2026: SNODAS up to 296 mm of snow water in the high Sierra, DWR's sensors mostly nil); both are kept.The record of water reaching the ground where it runs off unmeasured.adapters/nws_precip.py, adapters/snodas.py
4.6RecordEvapotranspiration over the Delta (OpenET)The Delta's lowlands are sampled: OpenET allows 50,000 acres a query, so six boxes of about 19,000 acres each, on a regular 3 by 2 grid across the area (not chosen by hand), are averaged. OpenET's days between satellite passes are its own estimates (from the day's reference evapotranspiration), so its latest days are partly estimated. Not yet compared with DSM2's island water use: that needs the Delta's irrigated area from a sourced land-use survey.The query limit.runner/soil.py openet_samples, adapters/openet.py
4.7SignsWater seen from radar (OPERA DSWx-S1)"New water" is water where, in the same season (30 days either side of the date) of the last two years, at least 3 passes saw the pixel water less than 20% of the time; patches of 0.5 km² or more are listed. Overlapping tiles fill each other's gaps. Radar can mistake wet fields and smooth ground for water; the bypass boxes take in the fields beside the bypasses.Many Delta islands are flooded on purpose in winter, so water alone means nothing.runner/water_extent.py, adapters/opera.py
4.4RecordSnowpackDWR's reviewed station values, not raw sensors; a basin's percent of April 1 is its stations' total against their April 1 averages' total.Raw pillows read inches where DWR's reviewed value is nil.adapters/cdec_reports.py
